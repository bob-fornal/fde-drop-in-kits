# Incident Root-Cause Analysis

Review authorized logs, stack traces, incident reports and relevant commit
history, avoiding secrets and personal data. Draft a blameless timeline with
timestamps and sources, impact, contributing factors, root cause supported by
evidence, detection and response, and preventative actions with proposed owners.
Separate hypotheses from confirmed facts, mark gaps and ask for missing evidence;
do not assign blame or invent an incident timeline.
